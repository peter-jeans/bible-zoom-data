# Data licence

Each data file draws on the sources listed in `seed/bible/sources.json`, which records every
source's licence and attribution.

- **Public-domain sources** (e.g. KJV, World English Bible, Strong's, Brown-Driver-Briggs,
  Matthew Henry) remain in the public domain.
- **Openly licensed sources** keep their own licence terms (CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0), and
  their attribution must be kept.
- **Link-only sources** are referenced by title and link only. No text from them is included.
- **Bible Zoom's own compiled annotations** (layer tags, structure, summaries) are released
  under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**, so they
  can be combined with CC BY-SA sources.
